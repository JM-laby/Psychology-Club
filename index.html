Curated Research References

APA-formatted sources, databases, and writing guides for PBS members.

Neuroscience

Walker, M. P. (2017). Why We Sleep: Unlocking the Power of Sleep and Dreams.

Scribner. Comprehensive exploration of sleep architecture, memory consolidation, and brain health.

Behavioral Psychology

Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, Fast and Slow.

Farrar, Straus and Giroux. Seminal work on System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, logical) cognitive thinking.

Cognitive Science

Sapolsky, R. M. (2017). Behave: The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst.

Penguin Press. An interdisciplinary look at neuroscience, evolutionary biology, and sociology shaping human action.
